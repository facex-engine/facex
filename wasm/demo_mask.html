How it works. Every animation frame we call sdk.detect(video), get a bounding box + 5 keypoints (eyes, nose, mouth corners), then position the mask at the eye-midpoint, sized to the inter-eye distance, rotated by the eye-line angle.

Lock to me. Saves a 512-dim embedding of your current face. After that, the mask only follows the face whose embedding is closest to yours (cosine similarity > 0.3). Other faces in frame are ignored.

Everything runs in the browser — no server, no upload.
